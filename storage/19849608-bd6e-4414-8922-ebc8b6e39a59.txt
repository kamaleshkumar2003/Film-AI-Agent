TITLE: NEON HARBOR
WRITTEN BY: PRODUCTION TEST SUITE

EXT. DOCKS - SUNSET

A torrential rain lashes against the rusted shipping containers. The setting sun bleeds deep amber and purple through the storm clouds.

A sleek black MOTORCYCLE is parked beside pier 42.

ARJUN (30s), wearing a leather jacket soaked through with rain, waits beneath the metal crane. 

MEERA (20s) rushes down the pier, clutching a waterproof BACKPACK. Her hair is plastered to her face, wet clothing clinging to her shivering frame.

MEERA
Arjun! We don't have much time!

ARJUN
Did you get the drive?

Meera unzips the backpack and produces an encrypted LAPTOP and a set of brass KEYS.

MEERA
Everything is on here. But Viktor's men followed me from the downtown terminal.

She checks over her shoulder into the misty gloom. A thunderclap shakes the harbor.


INT. ABANDONED WAREHOUSE - NIGHT

Darkness. Dust motes float through high broken windows.

Arjun and Meera step inside, closing the heavy steel sliding door behind them.

Arjun sets the laptop onto a wooden crate. He flips open the screen. Green code reflects in his eyes.

Suddenly, the red laser dot of a firearm dances across Arjun's chest.

HENCHMAN (O.S.)
Hands where I can see them!

TWO ARMED GUARDS emerge from the shadows. The lead Guard raises a semi-automatic PISTOL.

Arjun lunges forward! A physical fight erupts.

Arjun sweeps the Guard's legs. The pistol discharges with a blinding MUZZLE FLASH! The bullet ricochets off a steel pillar.

Arjun takes a blunt strike to the brow—a deep cut opens, and FRESH BLOOD streams down the side of his face. He disarms the Guard and sends the weapon clattering across the concrete.

ARJUN
Meera, run! Grab the laptop!


EXT. CITY STREET - NIGHT - CONTINUOUS

Tires screech on wet asphalt.

A black SEDAN careens around the corner at high speed, chased closely by a POLICE CRUISER with flashing blue and red sirens.

A CROWD of weekend bystanders and pedestrians scramble out of the way onto the sidewalks.

The sedan clips a parked delivery truck, sparking violently before regaining control and speeding toward the bridge.


INT. DETECTIVE OFFICE - MORNING

Bright morning sunlight cuts through dusty venetian blinds.

DETECTIVE CHEN sits behind a cluttered wooden desk covered in COFFEE CUPS and thick manila CASE FILE FOLDERS.

Her desk phone squawks on speaker.

CHIEF VANCE (V.O.)
Chen, tell me you have a lead on that harbor shootout.

DETECTIVE CHEN
We found motorcycle tracks and 9mm brass casing at pier 42, Chief.

CHIEF VANCE (V.O.)
Viktor is boarding a private jet in four hours. If we don't intercept him before international airspace, the state department will bury this.

Chen picks up a POLICE BADGE and her trench coat.


INT./EXT. POLICE TRANSPORT VAN - DAY

The police van rattles down the highway in heavy traffic.

Inside the rear cage, Arjun sits handcuffed. Meera sits opposite him.

Two tactical officers monitor a rack of DIGITAL RADIO EQUIPMENT.

OFFICER
ETA to central booking is fifteen minutes. Stay alert.

Arjun catches Meera's gaze. He discreetly wiggles the concealed brass keys from his pocket.


EXT. SKYSCRAPER ROOFTOP - DAWN

The city skyline awakens beneath a breathtaking dawn horizon. Golden hour light bathes the rooftop helipad.

A high-powered HELICOPTER hovers fifty feet overhead, its rotor wash whipping Arjun's jacket violently.

Arjun stands on the rooftop ledge, face bruised, holding the recovered drive.

ARJUN
(screaming over the rotor noise)
It's over! The data is already broadcast!

FADE OUT.
